② 整理得：Y(s) = \dfrac{b_{0}s + b_{1}}{a_{0}s^{2} + a_{1}s + a_{2}}X(s) + \dfrac{a_{0}y(0)s + a_{0}\dot{y}(0) + a_{1}y(0) - b_{0}x(0)}{a_{0}s^{2} + a_{1}s + a_{2}}
③ 反变换得时间解：
