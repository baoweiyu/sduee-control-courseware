⑴ 零、极点表示

G(s) = \dfrac{b_{0}(s-z_{1})(s-z_{2})\cdots (s-z_{m})}{a_{0}(s-p_{1})(s-p_{2})\cdots (s-p_{n})} = K_{1}\dfrac{\prod (s-z_{j})}{\prod (s-p_{i})}
z_{j} —— 零点（j=1..m）；p_{i} —— 极点（i=1..n）；K_{1} = b_{0}/a_{0} —— 传递系数

⑵ 时间常数表示

G(s) = \dfrac{K(\tau _{1}s+1)(\tau _{2}s+1)\cdots }{(T_{1}s+1)(T_{2}s+1)\cdots } = K\dfrac{\prod (\tau _{j}s+1)}{\prod (T_{i}s+1)}
Ti、τj —— 时间常数；K —— 放大系数
两种形式间的变换：T_{i} = -1/p_{i}\text{，} \tau _{j} = -1/z_{j}\text{，} K = K_{1}\cdot \dfrac{\prod (-z_{j})}{\prod (-p_{i})}
一般形式：含复数零极点、零值极点时，传递函数可分解为典型环节因子的乘积： G(s) = \dfrac{K}{s^{\nu }}\cdot \dfrac{\prod (\tau _{j}s+1)\cdot \prod (\tau _{k}^{2}s^{2}+2\zeta _{k}\tau _{k}s+1)}{\prod (T_{i}s+1)\cdot \prod (T_{l}^{2}s^{2}+2\zeta _{l}T_{l}s+1)} —— 任何线性定常系统都可看作若干典型环节的组合（下页起逐一认识）。
